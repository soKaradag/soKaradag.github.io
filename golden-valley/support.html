Support

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Serdar Onur Karadağ

Get help

Tell us your iOS version, game version and the steps that caused the problem. If a purchase is involved, include the product name and approximate purchase time. Never send your Apple password or payment-card details.

An emerald purchase did not arrive

Keep the existing app installed. Check your connection, then reopen the game; verified purchases that have not finished delivery are checked on startup and when the game returns to the foreground. If the emeralds still do not arrive, contact support. Refund requests are handled by Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com.

An ad is unavailable

Ads depend on availability, your connection and applicable consent choices. An unavailable or unfinished rewarded ad grants no reward and does not consume a completed daily claim. Try again later. Emerald and gold rewards each have their own three-per-day limit.

While you are away

Only depots with storage enabled fill while the game is closed. Full depots stop offline production. You do not earn offline gold, and the season clock pauses while you are away.

Protect your progress

Progress saves automatically on this device. Player accounts, save import/export and cloud sync are not available in this version. Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app to fix an issue, because local progress and consumable emerald balances are not backed up by a Golden Valley account.

No sound

Enable Game sounds in Settings, check your iPhone's media volume and audio output, and reopen the game if necessary. Music and effects pause for full-screen ads and should resume when the ad closes.

Languages

English, Turkish and Japanese are available in Settings → General → Language.
