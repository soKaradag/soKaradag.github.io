Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Serdar Onur Karadağ

About this policy

This policy describes how Golden Valley: Idle Mining, operated by Serdar Onur Karadağ, handles information when you play the iOS game or contact support.

Your game stays on your device

Mine ownership, gold, emerald balances, stored ore, quests, settings, reward limits and purchase-delivery records are saved locally. We do not run a player account, cloud-save server, chat service or leaderboard. Ordinary mining progress is not sent to a developer-operated server. iOS device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Purchases

Apple processes payments through the App Store. The game receives verified product and transaction information to deliver emeralds and prevent duplicate delivery. We do not receive your payment-card details. Purchase-delivery identifiers are retained locally to recognize transactions already credited. Apple's handling of your account and payments is governed by its privacy policy.

Advertising and consent

The game uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for rewarded and interstitial ads. Ad services may process IP-derived general location, device or app identifiers, ad interactions and technical diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent abuse. The app requests non-personalized ads, does not request Apple's advertising-tracking permission, and disables Google's publisher first-party ID. Non-personalized advertising can still involve data processing.

Your advertising choices

Where a consent message is required, the release app requests the applicable choices before requesting ads. You can reopen Ad privacy choices from Settings when that option is required and available. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad does not prevent ordinary mining. Ads and purchases require a network connection.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and investigate the reported issue. Send only information relevant to the problem. Support correspondence is kept while needed to resolve the request or meet applicable obligations.

Retention, deletion and rights

Local game data stays until you delete it or remove the app, subject to any device backup. Settings → Save → Start a new adventure lets you confirm deletion of local progress. This also removes local gold, emerald balances and mine progress. Deleting local progress does not erase Apple's purchase history or data already processed by advertising providers. Contact us about support data or applicable privacy rights; contact Apple or Google for information held by those services.

Service providers and international processing

Apple and Google process information under their respective privacy policies and applicable service terms. Their systems may operate in countries other than yours. Provider retention and deletion practices are described in those policies. We do not have direct access to their individual device records.

Children

Golden Valley is a general-audience game and is not designed specifically for children. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern, contact us so we can review it and direct you to the relevant service provider when necessary.

Support website

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising, cookies or contact forms to these pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to provide and protect its hosting service under its privacy statement. Sending an email uses your email provider and the support mailbox provider.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the app's handling of information changes. The update date appears above. For game support or privacy questions, use the support contact below.
